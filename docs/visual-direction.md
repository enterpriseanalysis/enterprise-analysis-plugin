# Field notes

An editorial grid gives evidence room to breathe. Large left-aligned typography establishes a clear entry point; small monospace labels supply the rhythm of a research instrument. Meticulously crafted spacing carries more weight than decoration.

Deep teal anchors the composition. Leaf green marks the point of action. Both come from the original EA monogram. Warm white and pale mineral surfaces keep the presentation calm, with painstaking attention to contrast at small sizes.

Repeated panels suggest comparable observations, while generous gutters keep each thought separate. The composition shows the passage from a business question to structured evidence without inventing a score, ranking or product screenshot.

Every visible word earns its space. Master-level execution here means precise alignment, restrained accents and useful hierarchy. The small-screen view must preserve the central message before it reveals the supporting details.
